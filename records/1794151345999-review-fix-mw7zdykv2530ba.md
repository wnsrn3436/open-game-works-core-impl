## done

- routing (server): an upgrade request that is not taken over is answered and then its connection is closed once the answer is written, the way `node:http` closes a `Connection: close` answer (`server/core/routing/pipeline.ts:885` `answer_and_close`). This applies to the path-table 404 (`:865`) and to a route that returns a status (`:871`)
- On a request worker the closed `BridgeSocket` posts `sclose`. The front then calls `done` and destroys the client socket after its writes are flushed when the worker had already ended its side (`server/core/routing/server_process.ts:488-492`). So the front no longer drops the end of an answer such as the matchmaker `refuse` (`end` and then `destroy`). The upgrade messages of `from_worker` moved into `from_upgrade` (`:482`) to keep lint complexity at or below 15
