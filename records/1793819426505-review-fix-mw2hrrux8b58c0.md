## spec proposals

- R1-8 and R1-10 (`spec change` `yes`, not fixed): the spec should decide whether a `recover_purchases` or `restore_purchases` that is still running from an ended login session is shared with a call from the new session. `payment-core.md:144` and `:161` say to share the running one, and `:160` discards its result. Recommendation: do not share a run of an ended session. A call in the new session starts its own run, and the `GOOGLE` restore of `:146` runs in the new session

## unverified

- R1-3 publishes `SessionEndedEvent` directly. In a first version that used `AuthCore.logout()`, the held `RecoverPurchase` did not end after logout: NetworkCore logged "AuthCore: access token: no session" and the test reached its 30 s time limit. So the R1-3 test checks PaymentCore's handling of the event, not an end-to-end logout while a request is held. Whether a held request must end after logout belongs to NetworkCore/AuthCore and was not checked
- `client/godot/core/bootstrap_core/dev/bootstrap_core_dev.gd:135` and `bootstrap_core/tests/test_boot_dev_run.gd:430`/`:448` still use `FakeStoreAdapter` by its global name. They only construct it or use `is`, and read no members. They are outside the PaymentCore target and were not changed
