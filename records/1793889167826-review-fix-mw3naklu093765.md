# review fix: live-event round 1

## done

- `live-event` (server/core/live-event/index.ts): `settlement_change` of `on_request_start` returns `false` only on `WITHDRAWN`. A settlement change whose transaction is `FAILED`, or whose `storage.run_user_transaction` threw, leaves its `error` log and the loop goes on to the next due participation. That participation keeps `settled` `false` and is settled again at the next request
- Test added: server/core/live-event/tests/settlement.test.ts:108 `settles the other participations in the same request when the hook of one breaks its transaction`
