| R1-3 | `correctness` | medium | client/godot/core/realtime_core/p2p_realtime_transport.gd:323 | The range makes a client that holds a match send `MATCH_LEAVE` of a second `MATCH_FOUND` and keep the first match, but the server already removed that connection from the first session. So a lost P2P connection in m1 cannot signal or rejoin, and A fails at the connect time limit of m2. The client follows open-game-works-core/skills/client-core/modules/realtime-core.md:298 and the server follows open-game-works-core/skills/server-core/modules/matchmaker.md:374, so the two specs together set this result.
