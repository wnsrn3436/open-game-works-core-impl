# update core: TweenCore set_finite_skip

## done

- `TweenCore` (godot): DEV operation `set_finite_skip` (`TweenCoreDev.set_finite_skip` in `client/godot/core/tween_core/dev/tween_core_dev.gd`). The skip is decided when the tween is requested (`play_value`, `play`, `play_preset`, including a preset that loads later) and stored on the record. A skipped tween that does not repeat forever publishes no `TweenStartedEvent`, does not progress, and ends with `Completed` at its end state in the deferred end-due flush (the same point where a cycle time 0 tween ends). Forever tweens and tweens requested before the skip turned on play as usual. `release` turns the skip off. A call before `init` or after `release` leaves an error log.
