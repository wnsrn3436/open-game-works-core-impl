## spec proposals

- R1-1 (spec change: yes, not fixed): tween-core `## DEV` sets the end time of a skipped tween to "the time a tween whose definition has cycle time 0 ends". So a completion callback or `wait_end` continuation that plays a finite tween again during the skip ends it again in the same deferred flush, and a chain that replays itself never lets the frame end. Proposal: add to `## DEV` that a tween requested during the skip from inside the end processing of a skipped tween (its completion callback or a `wait_end` continuation) ends at the next frame, not at the end time of a tween with cycle time 0. Add a `## test` item: during the skip, a completion callback that plays the same finite tween again ends once per frame, and the frame ends
