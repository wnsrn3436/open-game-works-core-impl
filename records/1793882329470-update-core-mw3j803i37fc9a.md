## done

- Module: `CameraCore` (Godot). Compared the implementation with the spec changes of 2e8185b..HEAD of `skills/client-core/modules/camera-core.md` and the CameraCore rows of `skills/client-core/engines/godot.md`. The code already followed: zoom applied in step 1 before bounds fitting, `snap_to`, a `move_to` with duration 0 and a `zoom_to` with duration 0 applying zoom, bounds fitting and shake inside the call, `set_bounds` fitting at the next frame, view range with the camera at the base position and base rotation, per-axis bounds fitting, trauma reduced before the offset is set, failed operations changing nothing, `move_by_preset` ending the follow at the call, `detach_camera` and `release` leaving the camera at the base position and base rotation with its zoom, `release` ending a running `move_to` and `zoom_to` with `Failed`, and the `Camera2D` settings at `attach_camera`

Doc comments of `attach_camera` and `detach_camera` updated to the base rotation and kept zoom
