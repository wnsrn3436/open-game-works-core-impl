## spec proposals

- R1-2: input-core.md `## settings` row of `InputCore.padAxisSensitivities` sets each element to be from 1 to 2147483647 (the Godot selected sensitivity and the `list_pad_axis_sensitivities` caller buffer are 32-bit), the `## error` `init` failure row for out-of-range sensitivities includes "more than 2147483647", and `settings/core-settings.schema.json` `InputCore.padAxisSensitivities` items get `"maximum": 2147483647`. Then `_read_sensitivities` (client/godot/core/input_core/input_core.gd) fails `init` for a larger element, and a `## test` item checks that `init` fails with [1000, 2147483648]. A smaller upper bound (e.g. 10000, ten times) would also fit the meaning of a sensitivity; which bound to choose is a spec decision
