With lines 2084-2085 removed, both tests fail ("the popup whose back policy is Close stays open"; "[2] expected to equal [1]: no exit confirm"), and they pass with those lines in place
- R1-2: fixed. `client/godot/core/ui_core/system_popup_view.gd`: buttons no longer get a fixed minimum height (`custom_minimum_size` 56 removed); they use `clip_text` with `OVERRUN_TRIM_ELLIPSIS` (line 70), so a label adds nothing to the button's minimum width. The row always goes in a row area: the `UISystemPopup` button area `Control`, or, when the area is taken from the root, a `ButtonStrip` child of the view along the bottom edge (same rectangle as before) (lines 84-99). `_fit_row` (line 127) runs on the area's `resized` and the row's `minimum_size_changed`. It sizes the row to the area and, when the row's combined minimum size is still larger than the area in either direction, scales the row down evenly so its global rectangle equals the area rectangle. Added `test_buttons_stay_inside_a_button_area_smaller_than_their_minimum_size` (`client/godot/core/ui_core/tests/test_system_popup.gd:122`): a 90×24 button area with three buttons, then long labels, then the area resized to 40×8; each check asserts each button rect is inside the area, in one row, evenly spaced and not overlapping. Added `test_long_labels_stay_inside_the_root_strip` (`tests/test_system_popup.gd:141`): very long labels in the code-made screen stay inside the root, along its bottom edge. With the old `system_popup_view.gd`, both tests fail (button rects 93×56 and 474×56 in a 90×24 area; 1964-wide buttons in a 1024-wide root), and they pass with the fix
